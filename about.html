About Feather Spoon Pantry

Slow cooking, honest ingredients, and everyday recipes from an independent kitchen. This is where I share the food I actually make — no shortcuts, no gimmicks, just deeply tested recipes and the stories behind them.

— Maren Ellison, recipe developer & writer

My story (and why I started this pantry)

I grew up in a small town in the Pacific Northwest, in a house where the kitchen was the heart of everything. My grandmother cooked on a cast-iron skillet that was older than she was, and she never measured anything — a pinch of this, a handful of that. She taught me that cooking isn't about perfection; it's about paying attention.

After culinary school, I spent a decade working in professional kitchens — from a bustling brunch spot in Portland to a Michelin-starred restaurant in San Francisco. I learned technique, speed, and precision. But I also learned that the food that mattered most to people wasn't the fancy stuff. It was the roast chicken on a Tuesday, the pot of beans that simmered all afternoon, the cookies that came out of the oven just as the kids got home from school.

In 2018, I started Feather Spoon Pantry as a place to document those everyday recipes. I wanted to create a resource for home cooks that was honest, thorough, and genuinely useful. Every recipe here is tested at least three times in a real home kitchen — not a test kitchen with a team of assistants, but my own kitchen, with my own family waiting to eat.

"The best recipes aren't the ones that impress your guests. They're the ones you make so often you don't need to look at the page anymore." — Maren Ellison

Today, Feather Spoon Pantry is home to hundreds of recipes, pantry guides, and slow-living kitchen wisdom. I write about the "why" behind each step, because I believe that understanding technique makes you a better cook — not just a better recipe-follower. And I still believe in good butter, anchovies, and the quiet magic of a well-stocked pantry.

What I stand for

🧂

Honest ingredients

I cook with real food — whole grains, seasonal vegetables, good olive oil, and salt that actually tastes like something. No weird additives, no unnecessary shortcuts.

⏳

Slow cooking

Not everything needs to be fast. Some of the best meals take time — a simmering pot of beans, a slow-roasted pork shoulder, a loaf of bread that rises overnight. I make space for that.

📝

Deeply tested recipes

Each recipe is tested at least three times, with clear instructions, metric and imperial measurements, and substitution notes. You can trust what you find here.

From my kitchen to yours

I love hearing from readers — whether you've made a recipe, have a question about an ingredient, or just want to say hello. I read every email, even if I can't always reply right away.

You can reach me at hello@featherspoonpantry.com, or follow along on Instagram and Pinterest for daily kitchen notes and behind-the-scenes.
