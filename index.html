Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Feather Spoon Pantry different?▼
Every recipe is tested at least three times in a real home kitchen. I focus on whole ingredients, clear instructions, and the "why" behind each step—so you learn to cook, not just follow.
Do you use metric or imperial measurements?▼
Both. Every recipe includes grams and cups/ounces, because precision matters for baking but flexibility is fine for soups and stews.
Can I substitute ingredients?▼
Absolutely. I include substitution notes in most recipes, and my pantry guides explain how to swap smartly without ruining texture or flavor.
How often do you publish new recipes?▼
Two to three times a week, plus a weekly newsletter on Sundays. Quality over quantity—each recipe is developed slowly.
