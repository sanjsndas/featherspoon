Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Feather Spoon Pantry uses cookies and similar technologies — and how you can manage them.

Last updated: March 15, 2025  ·  Effective: March 15, 2025

Feather Spoon Pantry ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website featherspoonpantry.com (the "Site"). This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and the choices you have regarding their use. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which describes how we handle your personal information more broadly.

By continuing to browse or use the Site, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, you can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings as described below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (they remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (they are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by the website you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a domain other than the one you are visiting).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes, including:

  • Essential functionality — to ensure the Site works correctly, such as remembering your cookie consent preferences.
  • Analytics and performance — to understand how visitors interact with the Site, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve the experience.
  • Preferences — to remember your settings and choices, such as search preferences or display options.
  • Third-party services — embedded content (like YouTube videos) and social sharing buttons may set their own cookies.

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising or to sell your personal data.

The table below describes the specific cookies you may encounter on our Site, their purpose, and their typical duration.

Note: Cookie names and durations may change over time as we update our services and third-party integrations.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, and they are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third parties. These include:

  • Google Analytics — for understanding site traffic and user behavior. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube — when we embed recipe videos. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Social media platforms — if you use sharing buttons or embedded posts (Instagram, Pinterest, etc.). Each platform has its own cookie and privacy policies.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your preferences in the following ways:

Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these options in the "Privacy" or "Security" section of your browser's settings menu. You can:

  • Delete all cookies already stored on your device.
  • Block all cookies from being set.
  • Allow only certain types of cookies.
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being placed.

Please note that if you disable or delete cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly. For more guidance, visit aboutcookies.org.

Opt-out tools

You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Consent banner

When you first visit our Site, you may see a cookie consent banner. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site, or by using the link in our footer (if available).

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers support "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals, which request that websites not track your browsing activity. There is currently no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals, and our Site does not currently respond to them. We will continue to monitor developments in this area.

7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
